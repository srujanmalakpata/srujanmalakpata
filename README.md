# Hey, I'm Srujan

Most people call me the Dude. Or Duder, or His Dudeness if you're not into the whole brevity thing.

I'm a CS student at the University of Calgary. I grew up taking machines apart, then taught myself to code and make games (I wanted to be a professional GTA V mod creator), and then the pandemic hit and I fell down a CS/math hole that I never really climbed out of. I'm not a mathematician, though. Math is more of a toolbox for me.

Mostly I'm just curious about how things work underneath: computers, AI, math, brains, language. I'm happiest when two of those collide, like when some strange piece of math ends up explaining why a program or a neural net behaves the way it does.

When I'm learning something, I like to poke at it until I get how it works and where it breaks, and I'm always happy to ask the dumb question. Same with code: I want it to read like a note to my future self, simple and obvious, with nothing clever just to be clever.

Away from the screen I'm still the same nerd. I watch a lot of movies, go deep on the lore of whatever universe has me at the moment (currently Warhammer), and listen to obscure soundtracks. I'm also trying to learn to make music, emphasis on trying. Brains, language, and the history of ideas keep pulling me in too, and I'll happily talk your ear off about any of them. Between all of that, I'm making some questionable long-term career choices.
